关于我们 About Us

我们是一家位于多伦多的专业装修与木工公司，由经验丰富的木匠领衔，拥有20年的行业沉淀。我们不仅提供全屋定制、木工工程、老房翻新，还擅长防水、水电、瓷砖、油漆等配套工程。我们的团队致力于为每一位客户打造高品质家居，确保每个细节都精益求精。 We are a professional renovation and carpentry company based in Toronto, led by experienced craftsmen with 20 years in the industry. We offer custom home design, carpentry, historic renovations, and excel in waterproofing, electrical, tiling, and painting services. Our team is dedicated to delivering high-quality homes with attention to every detail.
